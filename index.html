What to check

  1. A: did one click select the whole box?
  2. Did each paste keep the line breaks and the blank line ( ____ )?
  3. Did the bold heading paste as bold, or as plain text? (Plain is fine; just note which.)
  4. Did C's text box paste cleanly, with nothing from the page around it?
  5. D: did the table paste as a table, as tab-separated text, or as a mess?
  6. Which of A, B or C would you use under time pressure?
  7. E: did the note open over the page and close when you tapped elsewhere, or was it already showing?
  8. F: did the note open under the sentence and close on a second tap?
  9. E or F: which reads better at the point of care?
  10. G: did the full table fill the screen, did Close and Back both return you to G, and was neither table selectable?
